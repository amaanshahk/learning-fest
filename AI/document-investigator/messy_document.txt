PROJECT INVESTIGATION REPORT: NOVA SUPPORT PLATFORM

SECTION 1 — PROJECT OVERVIEW

The Nova Support Platform is an internal customer-support system developed by NovaTech Solutions. The project began in January 2024 and was originally scheduled for completion in December 2024.

The project team initially consisted of 12 developers, 3 designers, and 2 project managers.

The platform was designed to help support agents manage customer tickets, track incidents, and monitor response times.

SECTION 2 — PROJECT TIMELINE

According to the original project plan, development started in January 2024.

The first internal prototype was completed in April 2024.

The beta release was scheduled for September 2024.

A project update from October 2024 states that the beta had already been released in August 2024.

However, another internal note says the beta release was delayed until October 2024 because of database performance problems.

SECTION 3 — TEAM INFORMATION

The January staffing report lists 17 members on the project:
12 developers, 3 designers, and 2 project managers.

A June report states that the team had grown to 21 members after four engineers and two testers joined.

A later management summary states that the project team consisted of 18 people at its peak.

The personnel report from November lists 20 active members.

SECTION 4 — BUDGET AND FINANCIAL INFORMATION

The approved project budget was $480,000.

The finance department recorded an expenditure of $310,000 by the end of August 2024.

A September financial summary reports total spending of $365,000.

However, the annual project review states that the project had already spent $410,000 by the end of July 2024.

The annual review also estimates that the final project cost will be approximately $525,000.

SECTION 5 — PERFORMANCE

The first performance test reported an average ticket-processing time of 4.2 seconds.

A July engineering report recorded an average processing time of 3.8 seconds.

After a database optimization, the August report claimed the average processing time had fallen to 2.1 seconds.

A customer-support survey from September reported that users experienced an average processing time of approximately 5 seconds.

The engineering team attributed the performance problems primarily to inefficient database queries.

SECTION 6 — SECURITY INCIDENT

In October 2024, the security team investigated an unauthorized access incident.

The incident report states that the event occurred on October 14 and affected 37 customer accounts.

A management summary published later states that the incident occurred on October 12 and affected 27 accounts.

The security team concluded that compromised employee credentials were used to gain access.

No evidence of customer password theft was found according to the security report.

SECTION 7 — PROJECT STATUS

The December project report states that the Nova Support Platform was ready for production deployment.

However, an engineering note from the same month states that several critical performance issues remained unresolved.

The product manager's final update describes the project as successfully completed in December 2024.

A separate operations document says the production launch was postponed until February 2025.

SECTION 8 — IRRELEVANT INFORMATION

NovaTech Solutions operates offices in several cities.

The company cafeteria introduced a new lunch menu in March 2024.

The IT department replaced 46 office monitors during the year.

The company organized a charity run attended by approximately 120 employees.

These activities were unrelated to the Nova Support Platform project.

SECTION 9 — FINAL MANAGEMENT NOTE

Management considers the Nova Support Platform an important strategic project.

The project improved several aspects of customer-support operations, although the available reports contain conflicting information about its timeline, staffing, spending, performance, security incident, and production status.

Further investigation is recommended before using any single report as the definitive source of project history.
