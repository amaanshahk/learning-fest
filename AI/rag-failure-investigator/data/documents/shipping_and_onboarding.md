# AtlasDesk Hardware and Onboarding

AtlasDesk is primarily a cloud-based service. Customers do not need physical hardware to use the platform.

Customers who purchase the optional AtlasDesk DeskHub device receive an onboarding guide by email after their order is confirmed.

DeskHub orders are normally delivered within 3 to 5 business days after dispatch. Delivery timelines may vary depending on the destination.

The onboarding process includes account creation, team invitations and initial configuration. Standard and Enterprise customers can invite team members through the administration dashboard.
