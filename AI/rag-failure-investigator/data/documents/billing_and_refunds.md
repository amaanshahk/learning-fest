# AtlasDesk Billing and Refund Policy

AtlasDesk charges subscriptions monthly using the payment method registered by the customer. Customers can view invoices and payment history from the billing dashboard.

Customers may request a refund within 14 days of their initial subscription purchase. Refund requests must include the registered account email and transaction reference.

Renewal payments are generally non-refundable. Duplicate charges caused by a verified billing error are eligible for investigation.

A subscription cancellation does not automatically generate a refund. Customers must submit a separate refund request.
