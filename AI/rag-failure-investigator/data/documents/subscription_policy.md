# AtlasDesk Subscription Policy

AtlasDesk subscriptions are billed monthly. Customers can upgrade or downgrade their subscription from the billing dashboard.

The Standard plan supports up to 5 team members. Organizations requiring additional seats must upgrade to Enterprise.

Customers can cancel their subscriptions at any time. Cancellation prevents the next recurring charge, but access remains active until the end of the current billing period.

The Standard plan includes shared inboxes, team collaboration and basic performance reports. Enterprise customers receive advanced reporting and custom integrations.
