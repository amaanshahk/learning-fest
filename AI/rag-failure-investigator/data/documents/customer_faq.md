# AtlasDesk Customer FAQ

Customers can manage their subscription, update payment information and download invoices through the billing dashboard.

The Standard plan supports up to 10 team members. Customers can invite additional members from the team settings page.

AtlasDesk subscription purchases are covered by a 30-day refund window beginning on the date of the initial purchase. Customers should contact support with their transaction reference to request a refund.

If a customer forgets their password, they can use the Forgot Password option on the login page. A password reset link will be sent to their registered email address.
