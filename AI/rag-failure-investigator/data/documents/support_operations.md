# AtlasDesk Support Operations

AtlasDesk customer support is available through email and the in-app support portal.

The support team aims to respond to standard enquiries within 24 hours on business days. Enterprise customers receive priority support.

DeskHub orders are normally delivered within 5 to 7 business days after dispatch. Customers can contact support with their order reference to request delivery updates.

Customers requesting refunds should provide their registered email address and transaction reference. Support agents can investigate duplicate billing charges and account access problems.

Password reset links are sent to the registered email address. Support agents cannot directly view a customer's password.
