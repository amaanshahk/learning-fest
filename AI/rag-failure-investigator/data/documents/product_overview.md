# AtlasDesk Product Overview

AtlasDesk is a cloud-based customer support platform for small and medium-sized businesses. It provides ticket management, customer communication, team collaboration and performance reporting.

AtlasDesk offers three subscription plans: Starter, Standard and Enterprise. The Starter plan is designed for individuals and small teams. Standard provides additional collaboration features, while Enterprise includes advanced administration and custom integrations.

All plans provide access to the ticket management dashboard. Customers can access AtlasDesk through a web browser. Mobile access is available through the official mobile application.

The Standard plan costs $29 per month. The Enterprise plan uses custom pricing based on organizational requirements.
